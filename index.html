About

Cameras were my entry point. Storytelling became the bigger job.

I started by learning to shoot. Then I learned that the footage is the easy part — what decides whether anything lands is the idea, the first second, and knowing who you're talking to.

Born in Panamá, raised in the United States, working in Virginia. Eight years across the places where work has to survive contact with a real audience: a TEGNA newsroom on deadline, NASA education outreach, nonprofit campaigns, university recruitment — and my own feed.

That last one taught me the most. Building a creator brand from zero forces you to learn the difference between content that performs and content that converts, and I bring that read to client work instead of guessing at it.
