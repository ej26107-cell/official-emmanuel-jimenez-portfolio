About

Cameras were my entry point. Storytelling became the job.

I started by learning to shoot. I stayed for the part that decides whether anything lands: the idea, the first second, and knowing who's watching.

Born in Panamá, raised in the United States, based in Virginia. I've cut local news to air deadlines at 13News Now, produced recruitment video for the Virginia Space Grant Consortium, gathered guest stories for Operation Smile, and covered campus events at Old Dominion University.

Production is half the job. The other half is briefs, shot lists, interview schedules, feedback rounds and handoffs. At VSGC I direct a team of four interns and line up interviews with subject-matter experts, so the work ships on time and says what the program needs it to say.

My own channel, Manvel Jan, is where I test formats before I pitch them — and where I learned the difference between content that gets views and content that gets people to act.
